Goal (Anya): a less experimental lens+shield+faceted-ellipsoid projector solver, GENERAL across emitters, that gets close to the compliance and peak intensity of
`spec-hl-v2` (default automotive) and `sqm-hl` (newest SQM) on her HB3 scene `~/Downloads/reflector-scene-4.json` (FMVSS LB2V, 26 rows, Ø350×194 cylinder envelope, 1300 lm HB3 at x = −46).

## Findings (observations unless marked)
1. v2's lens pick (auto) = stigmatic plano-convex (curved face TOWARD the LED): perfect on axis, rms blur 3.8° at 10°, 6.7° at 20°. Its merit weights on-axis blur 6/4/2.5/…, so it wins although it is the worst off-axis (inference). Forced biconvex was better (8 vs 10 fails, 188 vs 168 lm; within noise).
2. Flat side toward the reflector + convex conic out (k ≈ −0.5…−0.8) blurs 0.3–0.7° across the whole 0–25° field and accepts tan 0.6–0.9 (meridional model, f 30–50; scan in the 10-07 transcript). Curved-in conics other than k=−n² are 2–5° everywhere. The engine agrees with the analytic lens (test-lens.js), including `front: −1` on the flat entry.
3. v2 placed every facet in the forward hemisphere of the LED (0 facets behind it, 3–31 mm out, median 8) although the HB3 emits 50.0 % rearward; its cone rule (tan ≤ 0.85·tanAcc to the focus) caps sideways facets at ~0.7 × (focus distance).
4. FIRST v3 (10-07, 150 facets, flat-in k −0.5, f 54 Ø73, 2 M-ray judge, noisy): 18 pass / 3 fail / 5 unsure, 410 lm in window, reach 54/53 m (v2: 8 fail, 188 lm). Facets 9–34 mm out (median 23).
5. Loss ledger of the f 26 design (model, reflected flux ≈ 1012 lm): through the lens 488 (48 %), absorbed by the shield 334 (33 %), lost in the lens (TIR/aperture) 121 (12 %), passed by the lens 69 (7 %). Engine ledger of the same design: 494 lm escape unused (the forward hemisphere has no mirrors), 176 lm TIR.
   Inference: images straddle the shield edge because a facet's image (filament length × magnification / f ≈ several degrees) is wider than the 0.4° the aims sit below the cut-off → aims must account for each facet's image size (axial filament: streak orientation follows the facet's azimuth, SQM finding 2/26).
6. Lens size sets the flux: lm in window grows with aperture (f 20 Ø27: 268 lm … f 54 Ø97: 568 lm); the proxy ranks long focal lengths first, the model score (fails/deficit) prefers short ones — selection must weigh lumens.

## Step 4 results (10-07 evening; the ladder numbers live in the table at the bottom)
What was built: spherical-k-means cells over the WHOLE emission sphere on ONE base ellipsoid (a continuous cup; letting every cell pick its own depth made 40 % of the light hit other facets) · per-facet footprints from the model · greedy "fill then fix" aim placement with the shield as a mask · opt-in lens-bypass facets (paraboloid + two-curvature widening; `bypass: auto|off`, off = sealed projector) incl. DECALS (flux ∝ area) for the sign-point floors above the cut-off · shield edge height searched against the model, left and right separately · optional aim polish.
7. ⭐ The model is right and the harness metric was wrong: the engine's per-facet `perFacet`/`lmOnTarget` only count the 25 m TARGET PLANE (±11° at 10 m size), so wide-angle facets "vanish". The judge's far-field window lumens match the model to 0.2 % (591 vs 592, 625 vs 625). Compare far-field grids (test-model.js), never perFacet, for wide beams.
8. ⭐ Joint Voronoi: polygons must come from ONE diagram over both classes' centres, else lens-path facets cover the bypass cells' directions (a ray from the LED to a bypass facet hit a lens-path facet first).
9. ⭐ The ceiling row `0.5U 1R–3R` (FMVSS) is a SUM OF MANY BROAD TAILS: the biggest contributor gives 600 of 10 000 cd; every lens-path image is 4–9° rms because filament (5 mm) × magnification (~3) / f (54) is that large for facets 10–40 mm from the LED. Only the shield can make a sharp edge. The edge height therefore has to be searched against the model (edge shift L 0.2°, R 0.5° on her scene): 3 fails → 0 (2 M rays).
10. ⭐ Peak intensity is capped by image size, not by flux: peak ≈ core flux / blob solid angle. v3 peak 23 kcd at 150 facets vs 43–53 (SQM / spec-hl): their facets sit 100–170 mm out (images 0.5–3°), v3's lens-path facets 10–40 mm out. Raising peak means moving the hot zone's flux onto far (bypass) facets or a bigger focus distance (limited by the envelope length: D = focus − LED = 63 mm at f 54).
11. The aim polish (pixel-level floors/ceilings + tracking, coordinate descent) cuts its own cost 46 k → 3 k yet makes the model's judge verdict worse (fails 3 → 6): the pixel cost cannot see the cut-off gradient / inclination rows and the Gaussian stand-in for wide bypass footprints is off. Kept as an option (`polishRounds`, default 1; best round by the judge wins, so it cannot hurt) — not a lever yet. Row-weight feedback and edge-facet freezing did not rescue it.
12. Lens-path efficiency on her scene (model ledger of the reflected flux): ~55 % reaches the window (shield 20–25 %, lens TIR/aperture 7 %, lens Fresnel 8 %); the bypass path loses only the mirror's 10 %.

## ⭐ Liou 2009 read (full text, pp. 1549–1555 of the PDF she found: 10.1243_09544070JAUTO1169.pdf; pages after 1555, the R112 results, NOT read yet)
Mechanism (H1 bulb = axial filament, ellipsoid reflector): in a conventional projector the ellipsoid's FIRST focus sits at one point of the filament, so the filament image straddles the second focus and the half above the cut-off line must be stopped by a screen (~half the light). Liou lets the first focus MOVE: Eq. (1)–(12) make the ellipsoid's semi-axes a(θ, Z) functions of the circumference angle θ and the axial position Z, so the first focus travels from one filament end to the other around the circumference (Fig. 16) and along the axis (Fig. 11). Zones whose first focus is at an end have their whole filament image on one side of the aim → under the cut-off with no screen; the six zones (Fig. 4: 1, 2, 6 stack the left, 3, 4, 5 the right; the horizontal cut-off is the border of zones 1/6, the 15° line the border of 4/5; 2 and 3 are buffers) are joined smoothly (no steps). Also: a reflector point farther from the filament makes a smaller, brighter image, nearer makes a larger, dimmer one — stack the bright small images where the light must stay, spread the big dim ones outward (Fig. 10).
v3 uses it as 'emitter-end focus' (`endFocus`, finalists only): a lens-path facet may take the filament's front or rear end (or a chip's edge) as its facet focus `S0`; the placement picks the alternative with the best fit. Clean A/B on her scene (150 facets, same layout f 42, 2 M rays): ON = 114 of 132 lens-path facets on an end, shield absorbs 125 lm (OFF: 247), through-lens flux 708 (598), traced window 597 lm (578), 24/0/2 (22/0/4). Not done from the paper: the smooth zone surface (v3's facets are patches on one base ellipsoid with a few-tenths-of-a-mm steps), the circumference/axis design equations themselves.

## Box experiments (10-07 late: is the lens limited by height or depth?)
The box fixture (56 deep × 105 wide × 55 tall, LED 33 mm behind the front plane) is DEPTH-limited: of 792 candidate layouts 390 are rejected 'focus less than 8 mm ahead of the LED' and 67 'focus behind the envelope'. Dragging the floor down 15 / 30 mm (a lens that hangs out below) changed nothing useful (14/0/5 · 565 lm; 9/3/7 · 627 lm vs 15/1/3 · 670 lm as is); pushing the FRONT forward 15 / 30 / 60 mm made the solver pick bigger lenses (Ø35, Ø53, Ø53) but gave no clear gain (12/1/6 · 508, 7/5/7 · 711, 16/0/3 · 539 lm; this box moves ±2 passes between runs, and the +30 run's cut-off aim looks mis-latched by the judge). Cause of the non-gain not found (guess: image size, not lens size). Sealed box (bypass off): 12/4/3, 356 lm (shield absorbs 392 lm of what the Ø23 lens receives) vs 16/0/3, 722 lm with bypass; bypass carries 527 of ~1319 usable lumens.

## Next version: cropped (rectangular) lens — Anya wants it
The solver interface allows it: a `quad` patch takes `clip: { kind: 'rect', hx, hy }`; a conic face of revolution is exactly `quad` with `curv: [1/R, 1/R, 0, (1+k)/R]`; the flat face is a `plane` with a rect clip; four absorbing planes close the sides; a toric face (Sun 2020) is `curv: [cx, cy]` with cx ≠ cy. Touches ~8 places in v3: the lens model's aperture tests (`traceLens`, the slab test in `optic.js`), `entersLens`, the shield half-width W, the bypass clearance (`lay.a + 2/4`), `directOk`, `cells.js` depth clearance, `ellipsoid.js`, `lensSurfaces`. Where it should pay: a LONG, THIN box with the emitter at the END FARTHEST FROM THE TARGET (room for a long focal length, but the round lens radius is clipped by the height; a parent lens with a_parent > 0.9 f cropped to the box height). The fixtures here are depth-limited, so there is nothing to measure it on yet — add a fixture (e.g. 200×60×40, emitter at the rear) first. Also decentred windows (the parent axis is not the crop's centre).
