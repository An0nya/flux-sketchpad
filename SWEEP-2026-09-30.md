# Facet-count sweep + fresh-target trial (2026-09-30)

Exploring-mode notes (Opus). Raw rows: `bench-out/sweep|calib|trial/runs.jsonl`, sweep table: `bench-out/sweep/table.txt`, images `bench-out/trial/img/` (all gitignored).
Runs: `tools/mode-run.js` with a timing split (solve · verify · prepare · trace · score). 4 workers; the solo re-run of 12 jobs shows parallel costs +10–24 % per solve and results are bit-identical.

## Timing (where a tuner's time goes)
- **Trace, 1M rays: 0.3–1.7 s**, grows mildly with facet count. **Scoring: ~10 ms.** Verify ~0.
- **Solve dominates** except for dish-fit under ~500 facets. Fill & fix has a **~2.5–4 s floor even at 5 facets** (default quality normal) → 6 s @100, 13 s @500, 25–39 s @2000. dish-fit: 0.1 s @5, 0.4 s @100, 3 s @500, 14 s @2000. SQM v0.3: 2 s @100, 35–38 s @500, 53–62 s @1000, and stops at ~1000 facets.
- Autotuners: dish-fit-auto costs 2.5–7 s (10–30× dish-fit) for ≤ +1.2 fidelity (−2 on p-hello@500). opus-mosaic-auto costs 9–45 s and gains a lot (lowbeam@100: 38.5 → 73.5).

## Facet counts (5…2000, solver defaults; fidelity / on paint)
- Fidelity plateaus; most of it arrives by 100–250 facets. default: dish-fit 99.1 @100, 100.0 @500; Fill & fix 96.9 @100, ~99 after.
- Fill & fix **places far fewer than the budget at high counts** (p-hello 742/2000, default 1383/2000, moon 1458/2000), and on p-hello its light on paint *falls* with more facets (29 % @10 → 14 % @2000).
- SQM on p-hello gets **worse** with more facets (59 @25 → 53 @1000; gaps 67 → 41).
- ofPossible > 100 % for Fill & fix / SQM on anya-lowbeam-212 (135 %): the peak cell gets more than the paint's share (light pulled into the hotspot).

## Fresh targets
- **Photos (moon, leaf, cat, flowers; the app's import, levels default): dish-fit wins every one**, at 400 and 1000, on both fidelity and the photo metric, with 48–67 % on paint. Eye-checked: cat recognisable (eyes, nose), moon clean; Fill & fix's moon shows far-field haze streaks (the fidelity blind spot).
- **Beamshot at 1000: Fill & fix wins** (bal-normal 87.1 / 72 %; dish-fit 52.6).
- **Cursive hello:** the stock 1 mm LED is the limit. **A 0.4 mm LED at 400 facets draws a legible "hello"** (text score 0.96, every loop kept; ff-bal-round-fast 74 fid / 41 % on paint, opus-mosaic crisper). A 1.5× envelope helps much less. bowl-image tops the text score on the stock scene but with 2–11 % on paint → the text mode needs a light guardrail.
- ⇒ The best solver depends on the paint type (photo → dish-fit, beam → Fill & fix, small-LED text → Fill & fix round / opus-mosaic). Argues for tuners that range across solvers.
